### Reference review

[GTM Engineeering](https://gtmengineeering.com/) presents a consulting/service model for venture-backed SaaS companies. It describes GTM architecture, ICP and buyer-journey operationalization, CRM/routing/attribution/reporting, workflow automation, multi-channel experiments, dashboards and fractional ownership of pipeline/RevOps initiatives.

Named tools, client claims and outcome figures are provider descriptions and were not independently verified. The site also contains mixed example/case-study content, so no performance figures were adopted.

### Captured direction

Build a technical GTM/RevOps agency for B2B SaaS that offers:

- Diagnostic and revenue-architecture sprints
- Fixed-scope CRM, routing, enrichment, attribution and automation implementations
- Multi-channel experimentation with shared measurement
- Executive/operator dashboards and metric definitions
- Fractional GTM engineering ownership and ongoing optimization
- AI-assisted research, signal classification, personalization, QA and reporting with human review
- Eventual productization of reusable data models, workflows and QA components

Keep this distinct from broad lead-generation, cold-outreach and AI-automation agencies because the offer owns the underlying revenue architecture and measurement layer.
