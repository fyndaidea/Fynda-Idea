## Captured direction

Build an independent marketplace and intelligence layer for SaaS, mobile apps and digital businesses with:

- Provider-backed revenue verification, provenance, freshness and stale-data states
- Structured acquisition listings and normalized revenue, profit, growth, churn and concentration metrics
- Buyer diligence rooms, seller-authorized evidence, identity checks, anomaly flags and transaction documents
- Category benchmarks, comparable-company multiples, filters, saved alerts and founder updates
- Permissioned API/data products for investors, brokers, accelerators and research tools
- Seller verification/listing plans, Buyer Pro, transaction support, workspaces and privacy-safe data monetization

The recommended wedge is profitable bootstrapped SaaS and mobile apps below a defined acquisition value. Differentiate on trust and transaction readiness rather than copying a public leaderboard.

## Reference review

[TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/) publicly presents a database of verified startup revenues plus a marketplace for SaaS, mobile apps and digital businesses. The reviewed site exposes provider/freshness language, leaderboards, acquisition listings, founder activity, benchmarking, API/MCP access and transaction resources.

The [linked Marc Lou post](https://x.com/marclou/status/2116155913904259373) dated October 30, 2026 says TrustMRR reached 4 million visitors, 208 acquired startups and $427,610 in revenue at 90% margin after one year. These are attributed founder claims and were not independently verified.
