## Reference review

The [AI Video Bootcamp](https://aivideobootcamp.com/) site presents a structured nine-phase AI image/video course and Skool community organized around a Learn–Practice–Earn loop. It advertises prompt libraries, templates, daily feedback, weekly updates, monetization paths and an Opportunity Hub connecting members to brand work, freelance projects, challenges and collaborations.

Membership, engagement, ranking and creator-result figures on the site are vendor claims and were not independently verified.

## Captured product direction

Build an original paid AI-video learning community that moves beginners from structured education through project practice and critique into verified portfolios and paid opportunities. Differentiate with skill credentials, brand matching, team formation and measurable job outcomes. Do not copy the source branding or proprietary lessons.

## Dedupe decision

Keep this separate from the broader **Nik AI Skool community** and from the **Skool/Circle alternative** platform product. This keeper is specifically an operated AI-video education, feedback and opportunity business.
