## Scope

Build a family of vertical operating products for dentists, law and investment firms, consultancies, hotels, restaurants, salons, contractors, HVAC companies and plumbing companies. Reuse a shared core for CRM, intake, scheduling, quoting, billing, payments, communications, reporting and AI automation, while tailoring terminology, integrations, compliance and high-value workflows to each sector.

Start with one costly, measurable workflow in one vertical, then expand toward the system of record after earning distribution and proprietary workflow data.
